# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Confirmed: the owner of the eToro portfolio, a private investor reading alone at a Windows desktop, who wants to know whether each holding deserves to be held, watched, or reviewed for trimming. Sharing with others and presenting live were offered and declined.

## Product Purpose
A local, offline report that turns the saved TipRanks data (analyst consensus and price targets, Smart Score, AI scores, bull/bear points, individual analyst ratings, technicals, news, earnings and fundamentals) into a decision aid for the owner's real eToro positions. Success: in a few minutes the owner sees what the data supports, what it does not cover, and which positions deserve attention.

## Positioning
Not a market-data terminal and not advice. A single-portfolio verdict sheet that joins the owner's actual positions and weights to third-party analyst signals through transparent, documented scoring rules.

## Operating Context
Data lives in `C:\Users\me\portfolio-data` (raw JSON, `portfolio.db`, `enriched_portfolio.json`); snapshot `20260930T203652Z`, taken 2026-09-30. Reports are generated by Python scripts, opened from disk, and printed to PDF.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- One self-contained HTML file plus a PDF copy; no network requests, no build tooling.
- No new TipRanks calls (3 of 50 free-tier calls remain).
- Every number traces to saved data; verdicts come from documented rules and are informational only.
- Crypto holdings (LTC, FLR, SGB) have no TipRanks coverage.
- Assumed, not confirmed: light/dark follow the system setting; English copy.

## Evidence on Hand
Everything under `snapshots\20260930T203652Z\` (`manifest.json` lists files and checksums). Absent and not to be fabricated: demo account, watchlists, trade history, TipRanks data for the copy-trading positions, the owner's tax situation, risk tolerance, and time horizon.

## Product Principles
1. Every number traces to saved data; say what is missing rather than fill it.
2. Keep what analysts say separate from risks the data cannot see (position size, taxes, horizon).
3. Lead with the answer (hold, watch, review), then let the reader verify it.
4. Weight matters: a verdict on a 0.03% position is housekeeping, not a decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Not established by the owner. Defaults assumed: WCAG AA contrast, meaning never carried by colour alone, keyboard-usable, print-friendly.
